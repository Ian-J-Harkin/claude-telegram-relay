# Gemini Telegram Relay — User Guide

Welcome to your personal AI assistant! This guide focuses on **how to use** your bot and its various features.

---

## 1. Interaction: Send a Message
Talk to your bot just like a person.

**How to use:**
Send a message to the bot in your Telegram chat:
> "What's the best way to cook salmon?"
> "Explain quantum physics like I'm five."

**Check it:**
- [ ] Does the bot respond with a helpful, conversational answer?

---

## 2. Personalization: Your AI Profile
Your bot gets smarter the more it knows about you.

**How to use:**
Send a profile setup command:
> `/profile I am Ian, I work as a designer, and I live in GMT time.`

**Check it:**
- [ ] Does the bot confirm it has saved your details?

---

## 3. Modalities: Beyond Text

### Photos & Images (Vision)
- **How to use:** Send a photo and ask "What do you see here?" or "Transcribe the text in this image."
- [ ] **Check it:** Does the AI correctly analyze the visual content?

### Voice Messages
- **How to use:** Send a voice note. The bot will transcribe it and reply.
- [ ] **Check it:** Does the bot understand what you said?

### Documents
- **How to use:** Send a PDF or text file and ask for a summary.
- [ ] **Check it:** Does it extract the key points accurately?

---

## 4. Memory: Facts & Goals
The bot remembers facts and tracks your progress.

**How to use:**
1. **Fact**: "I have a cat named Luna."
2. **Goal**: "My goal is to walk 10k steps a day."
3. **Recall**: Later, ask "What is my cat's name?" or "What are my goals?"

---

## 5. Specialized Features

### "Working on it..." (Live Progress)
For complex tasks, you'll see a status message. This means the AI is doing deep research or analysis.

### Action Confirmations (Safety)
If the AI wants to do something like "Draft an email," it will show buttons:
- **✅ Approve**
- **❌ Skip**
One tap and it proceeds or cancels.

### Voice Replies
- **How to use**: Send `/voice on`. The bot will speak its replies to you.
- **How to stop**: Send `/voice off`.

### Board Meetings
- **How to use**: Send `/board Should I move to a new city?`
- **What happens**: A team of specialized agents (Researcher, Strategy, Critic) will all give their perspectives.

---

## 6. Daily Routines
- **Morning Briefing**: A summary of your day sent every morning.
- **Check-ins**: Proactive messages to see how you're doing with your goals.
- **View Status**: Send `/schedule` to see when your next briefing is.
